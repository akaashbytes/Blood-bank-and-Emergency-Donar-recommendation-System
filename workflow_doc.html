1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

The LifeLink Blood Platform is a healthcare-grade digital network connecting voluntary blood donors, emergency hospital trauma units, regional blood bank vaults, and regulatory oversight councils across India. The platform strips away visual clutter to deliver high-speed, zero-friction blood unit allocation and cold-chain inventory management.

3. Role-Based Detailed Operational Workflows

3.1 Voluntary Blood Donor Journey

Target User: Registered or Guest Donors seeking to donate blood safely.

Stage Action System Output & Verification
1. Discovery Accesses public portal or searches regional stock. Displays real-time inventory levels for O+, O-, A+, B+, etc.
2. Screening Reviews health eligibility (Hb > 12.5g/dL, Weight > 45kg). Validates 90-day donation interval since last drive.
3. Booking Schedules appointment at accredited regional center. Generates Digital Donor Pass & QR Entry Pass.
4. Donation Visits blood bank for donation & viral testing. Updates total donation count & awards Gold/Silver Badge.
5. Certificate Downloads official voluntary donor certificate. Provides printable PDF certificate with reference code.

3.2 Hospital / Patient Requester Journey

Target User: Emergency ER doctors, hospital staff, or patient relatives.

Stage Action System Output & Verification
1. Requisition Fills Emergency Requisition Form in Portal. Captures Patient Name, Group, Component & Required By time.
2. Urgency Leveling Selects Critical Emergency (<1h), High, or Routine. Flags requisition with red alert tag in Coordinator Console.
3. Tracking Enters unique tracking code (e.g. REQ-2026-8812). Shows 5-stage timeline, ambulance ID & temperature log.
4. Delivery Receives cold-chain unit at hospital OT. Marks requisition status as FULFILLED.

3.3 Blood Bank Coordinator Console Protocol

Target User: Regional Nodal Officer managing vault stock and dispatches.

Module Operational Action Safety & Audit Rule
Dispatch Desk Reviews incoming emergency requests and allocates units. Verifies cross-matching and HIV/HBV/HCV negative certification.
Vault Console Monitors storage units (Vault A-1, Vault A-2 Cryo, Agitators). Triggers critical alert when units drop below safety threshold.
Expiry Alerts Tracks units expiring within 24h or 72h. Prioritizes near-expiry units for immediate allocation or transfer.
Donor Desk Screens donor health profiles and clears temporary deferrals. Maintains strict compliance with NBTC screening guidelines.

3.4 System Administrator & Regulatory Oversight

Target User: National Council directors and IT system administrators.

Feature Purpose Compliance Impact
AIP-160 Audit Logs Tracks user actions, IP addresses, and unit release approvals. Ensures immutable legal audit trail for all transfusions.
User & Roles Manages permissions for Donors, Requesters, Coordinators, Admins. Enforces strict role-based access control (RBAC).
Institutional Config Sets auto-dispatch safety margins (%) and hotline numbers. Configures automated SMS alerts and reminders.
